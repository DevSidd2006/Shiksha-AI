Poultry farming Rearing of egg and meat yielding chickens is called poultry farming. The objectives behind development of new hybrid varieties from a cross between Indian varieties like Aseel and exotic varieties like Leghorn are as follows : to produce good quality chickens in large numbers, to develop the ability to withstand high temperature, to use by-products of agriculture as poultry feed, etc. Rhode Island Red, New Hampshire, Plymouth Rock, Black Rock are varieties of chicken reared for eggs as well as meat.
