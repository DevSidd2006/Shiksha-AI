Universal indicators What are the colours of the following natural and synthetic indicators in acidic and basic solutions ? Can you recall? Litmus, turmeric, jamun, methyl orange, phenolphthalein ? We know that some natural and synthetic dyes show two different colours in acidic and basic solutions, and such dyes are used as acid-base indicators. In the pH scale, the pH of solutions varies from 0 to 14 in accordance with the strength of the acid or base. To show these variations in pH, a universal indicator is used. A universal indicator shows different colours at different values of pH.
