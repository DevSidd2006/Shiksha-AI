The length CP or CA in the figure is the radius of curvature of the mirror. Principal axis : The straight line passing through the pole and centre of curvature of the mirror is called its principal axis. PM is the principal axis of the mirror in the figure. Principal focus : Incident rays which are parallel to the principal axis of a concave mirror, after reflection from the mirror, meet at a particular point in front of the mirror on the principal axis.
