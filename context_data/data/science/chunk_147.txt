The concentration of H+ and OH- ions formed by dissociation of water is very low, only 1 x 10-7 mol/L for each ion. However, the electrical conductivity of water increases on mixing a small amount of salt or a strong acid/base in it due to their dissociation and electrolysis of water takes place. Electrolysis of water Activity : Dissolve 2g salt in 500ml pure water. Try this Take 250 ml of this solution in a 500ml capacity beaker. Connect two electrical wires to the Test tube positive and negative terminals of a power supply. Remove the insulating cladding from about 2cm portions at the other ends of the wires. These are the two electrodes. Fill two test tubes upto the brim with the prepared dilute salt solution. Invert them on the electrodes without allowing any air to enter.
