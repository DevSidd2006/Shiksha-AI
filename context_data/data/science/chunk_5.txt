You all will of course always actively pursue the objective of developing their creativity and imbuing them with a scientific temper. l You can use ‘Let’s recall’ to review the previous knowledge required for a lesson and ‘Can you tell?’ to introduce a topic by eliciting all the knowledge that the students already have about it from their own reading or experience. You may of course use any of your own activities or questions for this purpose. Activities given under ‘Try this’ and ‘Let’s try this’ help to explain the content of the lesson.
