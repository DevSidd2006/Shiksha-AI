When the light rays coming from an object enter our eyes, we can see the object because, while passing through the lenses in our eyes, the rays converge, and the image of the object forms on the retina of our eye. This image, formed by the light rays actually converging at a point, is a real image. A real image can be obtained on a screen. Concave mirror The image produced by a plane mirror is a virtual image.
