Removal of these wastes from the body is very important and it is the excretory system that carries out this function. The human excretory system consists of a pair of kidneys, a pair of ureters, the urinary bladder and the urethra. The functional unit of the kidney that performs the basic function of filtration is called a nephron. Each nephron has a cup-like, thin-walled upper part called the Bowman’s capsule. The network of capillaries in it is called a glomerulus. The urea produced in the liver comes into the blood. When the urea-containing blood comes into the glomerulus, it is filtered through its capillaries
