We have learnt about force and its effect on an object through Newton’s first and second laws of motion. ‘However, in nature force cannot act alone.’ Force is a reciprocal action between two objects. Forces are always applied in pairs. When one object applies a force on another object, the latter object also simultaneously applies a force on the former object. The forces between two objects are always equal and opposite. This idea is expressed in Newton’s third law of motion. The force applied by the first object is called action force while the force applied by the second object on the first is called reaction force. ‘Every action force has an equal and opposite reaction force which acts simultaneously.’ Use your brain power ! 1.
