For example, two samples of the compound copper oxide, CuO, were obtained, one by decomposition of copper carbonate, CuCO , and another by decomposition of copper nitrate, Cu(NO ) . From each of these samples, 3 3 2 a mass of 8g of copper oxide was taken and each was treated independently with hydrogen gas. Both gave 6.4 g copper and 1.8 g water. Let us see how does this is a verification of the law of constant proportion. The reaction of copper oxide with hydrogen yielded two known substances, namely, the compound water and the element copper.
