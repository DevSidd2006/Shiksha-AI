Na Cl NaCl with an ionic bond 5.1 Formation of the compound NaCl Electronic configuration The outermost shell of a sodium and a chlorine atoms is not a complete octet. However - outermost shells in both the Na+ and Cl ions are complete octet. An electronic configuration with a complete octet indicates a stable state. Further, an - ionic bond is formed between the oppositely charged Na+ and Cl ions and therefore an ionic compound NaCl having very high stability is formed.
