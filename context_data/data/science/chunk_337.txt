Principles of heredity are based upon this fact. If both the parents make equal contribution to inheritance of characteristics, which characteristics will appear in the progeny? Mendel carried out research in this direction and put forth the principles of heredity responsible for such inheritance. The experiments performed by Mendel, almost a century ago are quite astonishing. All of Mendel’s experiments were based upon the visible characteristics of the pea plant (Pisum sativum).
