Cells of meristematic tissue contain thick cytoplasm, a conspicuous nucleus and a thin cell wall and are compactly packed together. Vacuoles are usually absent in these cells. These cells are highly active. To bring about plant growth is the main function of meristematic tissue. According to the location, meristematic tissue is of three types as given below. Thus, they permanently acquire a specific structure, shape and location and perform a specific function. This is called as differentiation and permanent tissues are formed from these differentiated cells. There are two types of permanent tissues-simple permanent and complex permanent tissue.
