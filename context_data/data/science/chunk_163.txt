Figure 7.4 shows the energy transfer that takes place at each trophic level. There are different levels of energy exchange in the food chain. The initial quantity of energy goes on decreasing at every level of energy exchange. Similarly, the number of organisms also decreases from the lowest level to the highest level. This pattern of energy exchange in an ecosystem is called a ‘Pyramid of energy’. After the death of apex consumers, their energy becomes available to the 10 kcal decomposers.
