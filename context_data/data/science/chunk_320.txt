The spinal cord is protected by the vertebral column. In the space between the delicate central nervous system and its bony covering are the protective layers called the meninges. Cavities present in various parts of the brain are called ‘ventricles’ whereas the long tubular cavity of the spinal cord is called the ‘central canal’. The ventricles, central canal and spaces between the meninges are filled with cerebro-spinal fluid.
