Thus, if no external force is acting on two objects, then their total initial momentum is equal to their total final momentum. This statement is true for any number of objects. ‘When no external force acts on two interacting objects, their total momentum remains constant. It does not change.’ This is a corollary to Newton’s third law of motion. The momentum is unchanged after the collision. The momentum gets redistributed between the colliding objects.
