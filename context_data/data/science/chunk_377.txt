From early days, man has been curious about the sun as well as the moon and stars seen in the night sky. Using his boundless imagination, he tried to understand the sky as observed by the naked eyes. He noticed that the position of the stars changed with time and had something to do with the occurrence of seasons. As the knowledge of the cycle of seasons was necessary for agriculture, sky watching began to prove useful to him. The position of the constellations was also useful to sea goers for navigation.
