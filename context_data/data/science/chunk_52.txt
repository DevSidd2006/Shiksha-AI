Kinetic energy What will happen in the following cases? 1. A fast cricket ball strikes the stumps. 2. The striker hits a coin on the carom board. 3. One marble strikes another in a game of marbles. From the above examples we understand that when a moving object strikes a stationary object, the stationary object moves. Thus, the moving object has some energy, part or all of which it shares with the stationary object, thereby setting it in motion. ‘The energy which an object has because of its motion is called its kinetic energy’. The work done by a force to displace a stationary object through a distance s is the kinetic energy gained by the object.
