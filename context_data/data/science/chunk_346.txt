Down syndrome (46+1, Trisomy of 21st Chromosome) : Down syndrome is a disorder arising due to chromosomal abnormality. This is the first discovered and described chromosomal disorder in human beings. This disorder is characterised by the presence of 47 chromosomes. It is described as trisomy of the 21st chromosome. Infants with this disorder have one extra chromosome with the 21st pair in every cell of their body. Therefore they have 47 chromosomes instead of 46. Children suffering from Down’s syndrome are usually mentally retarded and have a short lifespan. Mental retardation is the most prominent characteristic.
